Page Aid

Ask questions about the page you're on through your own LLM key. Nothing leaves your browser except the page text you send to the endpoint you choose.

Quick setup, click a provider to fill the endpoint, then pick a model & add your key:
Keyboard shortcut (opens the panel)
Applied immediately, no Save needed. Format: modifiers+key ("Ctrl" means ⌘ on macOS; "MacCtrl" is the Mac control key). Also editable under Firefox's Manage Extension Shortcuts.
Collapse behavior (clicking the panel title)
Applies immediately, even to an already-open panel.

Local endpoints and non-listed hosts need host permission. It's requested the first time you save such a URL.
